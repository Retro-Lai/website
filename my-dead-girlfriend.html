My Dead Girlfriend Keeps Messaging Me On Facebook

After the death of his girlfriend, a grieving man can't bring himself to close her Facebook account.

Behind the film

This film was my return to videography. It had been a while since I'd been behind a camera, and I wanted a project that would get me back into it.

I adapted the screenplay from the creepypasta by natesw on Reddit, then wrote, directed, shot and edited the film myself.
