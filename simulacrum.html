Simulacrum

A grieving computer science student finds a strange floppy disk containing an artificial intelligence. What follows is an exploration of good and evil, innocent and guilty, and what it means to be human.

Behind the film

I started Simulacrum my freshman year of college and finished it my junior year. I wrote and directed it with the goal of capturing the feel of the 80s B-movies I love.

I designed the costumes and sets to feel authentic to the 80s as people actually lived it, not the idealized, neon-soaked version we usually see. Then I edited the whole film to look like it was shot on VHS.
